About this site

A running study log — short entries dated by when I read or worked through them. The goal is to make my own learning easier to revisit, and to give the writing itself a place to live.

Each entry has a title, a short summary, the date, the source it came from, and the actual notes. Topics are kept loose so I can filter the list later.

Notes from ongoing study. Updated as I learn.
