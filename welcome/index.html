Starting a study log

Why this site exists and how I'm structuring entries.

meta May 8, 2026

Context

I keep notes when I read, but they end up scattered — Apple Notes here, a markdown file there, a half-finished post somewhere else. I wanted one place that I could revisit later and that didn’t require a polish pass before I’d let myself save something.

What I took from it

The shape that seems to work:

  • Date first. A study log without dates isn’t a study log; it’s a wiki. Sorting by date is the whole interaction model.
  • Short summaries. One sentence on each card. If I can’t, the entry’s too big or I haven’t actually understood the source yet.
  • Topics, not categories. A loose tag list filters better than a tree.
  • Drafts allowed. draft: true keeps a half-formed entry out of the index without making me throw it away.

Questions / open threads

  • Will I want a “currently reading” surface, or is the dated list enough?
  • Tag merging when the list gets long.
